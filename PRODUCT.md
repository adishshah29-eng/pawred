# Pawred — Product

## What it is
A Hinge-style matchmaking web app that helps dog owners find a healthy, compatible mate for their dog. Owners like a specific photo or prompt on another dog's profile (with an optional comment); a like back makes a match and opens chat.

## Who it's for
Regular dog owners in India (not professional kennels), usually on their phone in the evening, nervous about doing breeding responsibly and wary of random classifieds and WhatsApp groups.

## What's uniquely true
- Health first: vaccination status, health checks and optional pedigree papers live on every profile; an admin verifies documents before a Verified badge appears.
- Matching rules built for breeding: opposite sex only, same breed by default, minimum age 18 months.
- Likes are specific (this photo, this prompt), not blind swipes.

## Brand commitments (user-pinned)
- Feel: warm and editorial, like Hinge. Calm, trustworthy, premium. Dog photography is the hero.
- One warm accent color.
- Tone: friendly and plain-spoken, never cutesy; no puns in every headline.

## Surfaces and modes
- Marketing (landing, how it works, safety, breed pages): Persuade. Expressive, animated (Animmaster components live here only).
- App (onboarding, discover, likes, matches, chat, settings): Operate. Quiet, fast, consistent.
- Admin: Operate.

## Constraints
- Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind; college mini-project (DJS23AMD302L) that must visibly cover all six syllabus units.
